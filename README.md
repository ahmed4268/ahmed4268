## What I actually work on

I sit where hardware meets the web. Most of my day is one of these:

- **RFID middleware** — a Spring Boot service running on-site next to fixed UHF
  readers, buffering reads into local H2 so a dropped uplink loses nothing, and
  streaming them to the cloud over Kafka.
- **Multi-tenant SaaS** — hotels, insurers, factories. Tenant isolation, feature
  flags per subscription, and the boring correctness that keeps two clients'
  data apart.
- **The clients on top** — React and Next.js dashboards, Flutter apps for people
  who are holding a scanner rather than sitting at a desk, and a guest PWA with
  nothing to install.
- **The infrastructure under it** — OVH to Azure migration, GitHub Actions CI/CD,
  Nginx and PM2 with SSL, and a self-managed 3-node Kubernetes cluster on
  OpenStack for good measure.

Shipped to production for **GAT Assurances**, **Transcom**, **SOTIPAPIER** and
**Lutex**.

| Project | What it is | Stack |
|---|---|---|
| **[RFIDIA Asset](https://asset.rfidia.com)** | Fixed asset management with live indoor positioning — assets on a real floor plan, with depreciation and accounting inventory on the same record | `Spring Boot` `Node.js` `MongoDB` `React` `Flutter` `Kafka` |
| **[Lutex Fabric Tracking](https://stock.rfidia.com)** | RFID traceability for fabric piles across ten production zones, cutting room to loading dock | `Spring Boot` `Express` `Kafka` `Next.js` `Flutter` |
| **SOTIPAPIER Bobines** | Paper reel traceability across a chessboard-mapped warehouse — ~60 tonnes, moved by forklift | `Spring Boot` `Node.js` `React` `Flutter` |
| **[RFIDIA Smart Hotel](https://hotel.rfidia.com)** | Multi-tenant cashless RFID wristband payments — admin dashboard, guest PWA, Flutter POS, and a B2B till-integration API | `Spring Boot` `MySQL` `React` `Flutter` `NFC` |
| **Lutex Production** | Piecework MES for the garment floor — its real job is refusing submissions that do not add up, and logging why | `Express` `MySQL` `PDFKit` `ExcelJS` |
| **RFIDIA Business Suite** | Finance, CRM and HR as three platforms behind one account and one sign-on | `Spring Boot` `React` `SQL` `SSO` |
